Theory HOLCF-extra.Domain_Order_Derive

theory Domain_Order_Derive
  imports Domain_Order "HOL-Eisbach.Eisbach"
begin

section ‹Deriving order-class instances of domain-package types›

text ‹Proof methods packaging the derivation of @{file "Domain_Order.thy"}: for a type ‹T› defined
  by the domain package, a map rule ‹P v ⟹ P (T_map⋅v)› (one premise per type parameter) is
  proved by fixed-point induction on the map functional, from the package's ‹T_map_def› and
  ‹T.iso_rews›; the instance then follows at ‹v = ID› from ‹T_map_ID›.  The rules for the
  combinators are collected in named theorem lists, so map rules of earlier types can be
  registered for nested recursion.›

subsection ‹Fixed-point induction on a map definition›

text ‹The package defines ‹T_map ≡ Λ v⇩1 … v⇩n. μ f. G v⇩1 … v⇩n f›.  One beta step turns an
  equation ‹M = (Λ v. B v)› into ‹M⋅v = B v›, again an equation of the same shape with one
  ‹Λ› fewer; its continuity side condition is a single goal for @{attribute cont2cont}.  After
  ‹n› steps the equation reads ‹T_map⋅v⇩1⋅…⋅v⇩n = μ f. G f›, with the parameters free in ‹G›,
  and the induction rule below needs nothing more.  The equation is its first premise, for
  @{text erule}; the others come easiest first, ending with the step.›

lemma map_beta_step: "M = (Λ v. B v) ⟹ cont B ⟹ M⋅v = B v"
  by simp

lemma map_fix_induct:
  fixes G :: "('a::pcpo → 'a) ⇒ 'a → 'a"
  assumes eq: "m = fix⋅(Λ f. G f)" and adm: "adm P" and bot: "P ⊥"
    and cont: "cont G"
    and step: "⋀f. P f ⟹ P (G f)"
  shows "P m"
  unfolding eq by (rule fix_preserves[OF adm bot], rule preserves_LAM[OF cont], erule step)

text ‹For a non-recursive type the functional ignores its argument, and the fixed point is the
  body itself; this needs neither admissibility nor the ‹⊥› case.  The rule only unifies when the
  body does not mention the bound variable.›

lemma map_fix_const: "m = fix⋅(Λ f. c) ⟹ P c ⟹ P m"
  by (simp add: fix_const)

subsection ‹Registries›

text ‹The classes handled here (bounded completeness, separation, distributivity) are categories
  of domains closed under the domain constructions; the registries are prefixed accordingly.
  Each combinator rule mentions only its own predicate; what a property needs from the others
  (distributivity of ‹⊗› needs separated, bounded-complete components) is a sort constraint, so
  the classes are derived in dependency order.›

named_theorems domain_category_adm ‹admissibility of the predicates›
  and domain_category_bottom ‹the predicates at @{term ⊥}›
  and domain_category_iso ‹transport along an isomorphism, premises in the shape of ‹T.iso_rews››
  and domain_category_map ‹the predicates through the map combinators and registered type maps›
  and domain_category_ID ‹the predicates at @{term ID}, under class assumptions›
  and domain_category_deflation ‹each predicate implies @{const deflation}›
  and domain_category_class ‹class introduction from the predicate at @{term ID}›

lemmas [domain_category_adm] = adm_bounded_complete_defl adm_filter_sum_defl
  adm_separated_sum_defl adm_distributive_defl

lemmas [domain_category_bottom] = bounded_complete_defl_bottom filter_sum_defl_bottom
  separated_sum_defl_bottom distributive_defl_bottom

lemmas [domain_category_iso] = bounded_complete_defl_iso filter_sum_defl_iso
  separated_sum_defl_iso distributive_defl_iso

lemmas [domain_category_map] =
  bounded_complete_defl_u_map bounded_complete_defl_ssum_map bounded_complete_defl_sprod_map
  filter_sum_defl_u_map filter_sum_defl_ssum_map filter_sum_defl_sprod_map
  separated_sum_defl_u_map separated_sum_defl_ssum_map separated_sum_defl_sprod_map
  distributive_defl_u_map distributive_defl_ssum_map distributive_defl_sprod_map

lemmas [domain_category_ID] = bounded_complete_defl_ID filter_sum_defl_ID separated_sum_defl_ID
  distributive_defl_ID deflation_ID

lemmas [domain_category_deflation] = bounded_complete_defl.deflation filter_sum_defl.deflation
  separated_sum_defl.deflation distributive_defl.deflation

lemmas [domain_category_class] = bounded_complete_class_deflI filter_sum_class_deflI
  separated_sum_class_deflI distributive_cpo_class_deflI

subsection ‹Methods›

text ‹Each step of a method is a single rule application, mostly out of a registry, and every
  leaf goal is closed under ‹solves›, so a step can only succeed by doing what it is meant to.
  For a type ‹T› the per-type inputs are all generated by the domain package (‹T_map_def›,
  ‹T.iso_rews›, ‹T_map_ID›); no constructor-level lemmas are needed.  A typical use:
  @{theory_text [display]
‹lemma distributive_defl_T_map [domain_category_map]:
  "distributive_defl v ⟹ distributive_defl w ⟹ distributive_defl (T_map⋅v⋅w)"
  by (domain_category_map_rule map_def: T_map_def iso_rews: T.iso_rews)

instance T :: ("{domain, distributive_cpo}", "{domain, distributive_cpo}") distributive_cpo
  by (domain_category_instance map_ID: T_map_ID)›}
  where the ‹bounded_complete› and ‹filter_sum› instances of ‹T› are derived before, if the
  recursion passes through ‹⊗›.

  ‹domain_category_map_rule› proceeds as follows.
  ▪ ‹T_map_def› is inserted as an equation and beta-reduced one parameter at a time by
    @{thm [source] map_beta_step}, each continuity side goal closed by ‹intro cont2cont›.  Any
    arity works, including zero.
  ▪ ‹erule map_fix_induct› matches the reduced equation and the conclusion ‹P m› at once, which
    also fixes the parameters left schematic by the beta steps.  ‹P› comes from higher-order
    unification; a wrong unifier (e.g.\ a constant ‹P›) cannot pass ‹solves ‹rule
    domain_category_adm››.
  ▪ For a non-recursive type, ‹erule map_fix_const› is tried first: it unifies only when the
    functional ignores its argument, and then the predicate needs no admissibility or ‹⊥› rule.
    So non-admissible predicates (e.g.\ finitary levels) can still be pushed through
    non-recursive types.
  ▪ The iso rules take the ‹abs_iso›/‹rep_iso› equations of ‹T.iso_rews› as premises, so the
    registries list source theorems as they are, without ‹OF› or ‹rotated›.
  ▪ ‹intro domain_category_map domain_category_ID› decomposes the rep type; a combinator rule
    whose sort constraints the component types do not meet simply does not apply.  Side goals
    are closed by the induction hypothesis or by its deflation part; a non-recursive type
    without parameters leaves none.
  ▪ ‹declares cont2cont› lets callers add continuity rules.

  ‹domain_category_instance› rewrites ‹ID› to ‹T_map⋅ID⋅…›, finds the map rule @{attribute domain_category_map}, and closes the parameter premises from @{attribute domain_category_ID}.  What the user still decides by hand: the parameter sorts of
  each instance, and the order of the derivations.›

method domain_category_side =
  (assumption | erule domain_category_deflation)

text ‹Beta-reduce the inserted map definition, one parameter per step.›

method domain_category_beta declares cont2cont =
  ((drule map_beta_step, solves ‹intro cont2cont›)+)?

method domain_category_map_rule uses map_def iso_rews declares cont2cont =
  (insert map_def[THEN meta_eq_to_obj_eq], (domain_category_beta cont2cont: cont2cont),
   (erule map_fix_const |
    erule map_fix_induct,
     solves ‹rule domain_category_adm›,
     solves ‹rule domain_category_bottom›,
     solves ‹intro cont2cont›),
   rule domain_category_iso, solves ‹rule iso_rews›, solves ‹rule iso_rews›,
   intro domain_category_map domain_category_ID, (domain_category_side+)?)

text ‹Instance from the registered map rule: the class property is the predicate at ‹ID›, and
  ‹T_map⋅ID = ID›.›

method domain_category_instance uses map_ID =
  (rule domain_category_class, subst map_ID[symmetric], rule domain_category_map,
   ((intro domain_category_ID)+)?)

subsection ‹Future work: a derivation command›

text ‹A command ‹derive_order_instances T›, for one type or a mutually recursive group, would
  automate the remaining manual choices.
  ▪ ∗‹Package data.›  Read the type constructors, ‹T_map_def›, ‹iso_rews›, ‹T_map_ID› and the
    rep-type functional from the domain package's theory data.
  ▪ ∗‹Variants.›  Walk the functional and collect, per combinator position, the applicable
    rules: the general ones and the registered map rules of nested types.  Sort constraints at
    closed concrete types are decided by ‹Sign.of_sort›; those at types mentioning parameters
    become requirements on the parameter sorts.
  ▪ ∗‹Order.›  Derive the classes in dependency order (bounded completeness and separation
    before distributivity), registering each instance before the next derivation.
  ▪ ∗‹Arity.›  Each variant at ‹v = ID› gives a candidate arity; emit the unique minimal one
    (least parameter sorts), or fall back to the all-general variant with a warning.
  ▪ ∗‹Proof.›  Instantiate the stored rules directly, building the closed map equation from the
    known parameters and abstracting ‹P› over the map term instead of relying on the unifier.
  Mutual recursion runs the fixed-point induction on the tuple of maps, with the predicate
  conjoined componentwise; the registry rules are unchanged.

  Open: there are no rules for ‹cfun_map› or the powerdomain maps; prior art to mirror is the
  domain package's own ‹domain_take_proofs.ML›, AFP ∗‹Deriving›, and the relator properties of
  Transfer/Lifting.›

end
